Call basics (not legal advice)

  • Check the National Do Not Call Registry, your state's list, and our own do-not-call list before dialing.
  • Call only between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. in the prospect's local time.
  • Say your name and titlepen.com right away, and be honest about why you are calling.
  • If someone says "don't call me again," thank them, end the call, and write their number down on the do-not-call list right then.
  • Never promise results, savings, or that a draft is legally compliant.
  • Telemarketing rules differ by state, so ask a lawyer to confirm the rules for the states you call.
